BlackBox Privacy Vault

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to BlackBox Privacy Vault ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your digital privacy.

BlackBox is designed as an offline-first, zero-knowledge application. Your privacy is built into the architecture of the application rather than relying on centralized data collection.

1. Zero Data Collection & Offline Architecture

No Cloud Storage by Default: All photos, videos, notes, and files stored within BlackBox are kept locally on your device.

No Server Transmission: We do not operate central servers that receive, collect, process, or back up your personal vault contents. Your vault data is designed to remain on your device.

No Third-Party Trackers: BlackBox does not integrate third-party advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or data-harvesting software into the vault experience.

2. Local Encryption & Security Standards

BlackBox uses local encryption and device security mechanisms to protect the contents of your private vault.

Encryption

Media files stored in the vault are encrypted using AES-256-GCM, providing confidentiality and integrity protection.

Hardware-Backed Keys

Where supported by the operating system and device, cryptographic keys may be generated and protected using hardware-backed security mechanisms such as Android Keystore or Apple Secure Enclave.

Blind File Naming

Files may be renamed using randomized identifiers to reduce exposure of meaningful filenames at the filesystem level.

EXIF metadata such as GPS coordinates and timestamps may be removed from media during import when the application performs metadata sanitization.

3. Device Permissions

BlackBox requests only the permissions necessary for its intended functionality.

  • Biometric Authentication: Used through local device authentication APIs to protect access to the vault. Biometric templates remain under the control of the device's biometric security system.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used to allow you to select media and files that you choose to import into the private vault.
  • Screen Security: Where supported, screen-security mechanisms such as Android FLAG_SECURE may be used to reduce the risk of screenshots, screen recordings, and app-switcher previews exposing vault content.

4. Security Triggers & The "Nuclear Option"

BlackBox may include a security monitor, referred to as Sentinel, designed to detect certain device-integrity conditions such as rooting, jailbreaking, debugging, or other potentially unsafe environments.

If the application determines that a severe security compromise or tampering condition has occurred, it may initiate an emergency protection mechanism.

Depending on the implementation, this mechanism may invalidate or erase locally stored cryptographic keys and/or encrypted vault data to reduce the possibility of forensic extraction.

Important: Security-triggered deletion may be irreversible. Users should maintain any independent backups of important files outside the BlackBox vault if such backups are supported by their intended workflow.

5. Children's Privacy

BlackBox does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through a centralized collection system.

We do not knowingly target children under the age of 13 for personal-data collection.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application's architecture, functionality, security mechanisms, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
